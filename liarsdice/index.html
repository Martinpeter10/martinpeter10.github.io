How to Play

A new table every day - outlast 3 opponents to win it.

Rules

  • Everyone shakes a cup of 5 dice. You only see your own.
  • Players take turns bidding on how many dice of one face are on the whole table - for example "four 3s" means at least four 3s among ALL dice.
  • Each bid must raise the last one: either a higher quantity (any face), or the same quantity with a higher face.
  • Ones are not wild - every face only counts as itself.
  • Instead of bidding, you can call LIAR! All dice are revealed: if the bid was good, the caller is out - if it was a bluff, the bidder is out.
  • One wrong call and you're out for the day. Survive every challenge and be the last one standing to win.
  • After each elimination the remaining players reshake and a new round begins.

Example

Jon bids three 4s

You raise to three 5s (same amount, higher face)

David raises to five 2s (higher amount, any face)

You call LIAR! Only three 2s show up - David was bluffing and is out!
