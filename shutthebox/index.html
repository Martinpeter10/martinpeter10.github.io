How to Play

The classic pub game - flip every tile to shut the box.

Rules

  • The box starts with tiles 1 through 9 standing up.
  • Roll the dice, then tap tiles that add up exactly to your roll. Rolled a 7? Flip the 7, or 3 + 4, or 1 + 2 + 4 - your choice.
  • Once tiles are down, they stay down. Keep rolling and flipping.
  • Once 7, 8 and 9 are all shut, you may roll just one die instead of two.
  • When no combination of standing tiles matches your roll, the game ends. Your score is the sum of tiles left standing - lower is better.
  • Flip all 9 and you've Shut the Box - a perfect 0!

Example

🎲 You roll 4 + 5 = 9

👆 You tap 2 and 7 (2 + 7 = 9) - both flip down

🎲 You roll 8, but only 1, 3 and 5 are standing - no combo makes 8

📦 Game over: 1 + 3 + 5 = 9 points

One box per day - everyone gets the same dice. Tomorrow the box resets.
