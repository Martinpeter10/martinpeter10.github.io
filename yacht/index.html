How to Play

One scorecard a day - make every box count.

Rules

  • You get 13 turns - one for each box on the scorecard.
  • Each turn you roll 5 dice, then may reroll twice. Tap any die to hold it between rolls.
  • When you are happy - or out of rolls - pick a box to score into. Every box must be filled, so a bad roll sometimes means taking a zero.
  • Once a box is used it is locked for the day.
  • Score 63 or more in the Upper section for a +35 bonus.
  • One scorecard per day. When the last box is filled, that is your score - come back tomorrow for a fresh card.

Scoring

  • Ones - Sixes: add up only that number
  • Three / Four of a Kind: total of all 5 dice
  • Full House: 25 (three of one, two of another)
  • Small Straight: 30 (four in a row)
  • Large Straight: 40 (five in a row)
  • Yacht: 50 (all five the same)
  • Chance: total of all 5 dice
